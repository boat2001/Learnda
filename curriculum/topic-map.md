# Learnda proposed topic outline: KG 1–SHS 3

Authored 10 October 2026. **Planning draft: curriculum alignment and term sequencing are not verified.**

This outline covers every subject in the Learnda development guide, for each applicable class and Term 1, Term 2 and Term 3. Each cell contains two ordered broad topic blocks. Expand these into specific topics, objectives, lessons and practice after source review. These blocks are not a complete list of official curriculum standards.

## Review requirements

- Broad authored topic blocks, not an exhaustive or verified transcription of NaCCA standards.
- Year placement and term order are proposed and require curriculum and scheme-of-learning review.
- Reference links identify curriculum research starting points; they do not verify individual topics.
- Strands, standards, indicators, objectives and page references remain unfilled until checked against source documents.
- KG learning areas may overlap integrated themes; these are Learnda navigation categories.
- Primary Computing and French from B1 reflect Learnda product scope; official grade coverage requires verification.
- Ghanaian-language content requires a named language and its own orthography; Arabic and French require entry-level review.
- Intervention topics require diagnostic assessment and individual pacing.
- SHS subject access depends on pathway, school availability and combination rules. A topic entry does not grant access.
- SHS cohort curriculum versions, specialist options and prescribed literature texts require separate verification.

Revision and assessment should be planned alongside each term rather than treated as substitute curriculum topics. KG delivery remains play-based and parent-guided, without quizzes or exams. Food, workshop, sport and science activities need age-appropriate supervision and lesson-level safety planning.

## Implementation and verification

The matching `topic-map.json` uses canonical class codes, level-qualified subject keys and stable draft topic IDs. It contains no database UUIDs and is not automatically published or seeded. `topic-outline-data.cjs` is the editable source; run `node curriculum/build-topic-map.cjs` from the project root to validate coverage and regenerate both outputs.

For each topic block, locate the official subject document and class section, record its strand and sub-strand, add exact content-standard and indicator codes with source pages, refine the title and write measurable objectives. Confirm school term order separately. Split broad blocks into teachable topics while retaining a parent-block reference. Review pathway rules, language variants, cohort versions and specialist options before learner publication.
